With a plantnet pretrained resnet, we ran anthony's new baseline training script and achieved 61% accuracy.

This new architecture presented the opportunity to use the treatment-labled images more effectively. One of the biggest improvements was thanks to PlantNet, and 448/224 global scales + 9 overlapping 224^2 tiles.

In a 2-stage process, see if taking this PlantNet-pretrained Resnet18 can be finetuned to predict the treatment on these hundreds of images.
Then, finetune it further on the 209 ICP'd images to see how performance improves -- if at all -- versus the original 61% PlantNet ICP baseline.
