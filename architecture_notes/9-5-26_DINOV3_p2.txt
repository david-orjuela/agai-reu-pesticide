More here:
"The "Squeezed Middle" and Ordinal Dynamics
Analyzing the per-class recall exposes the geometric reality of binning a continuous chemical variable:
Class	Ground Truth Count	Correct Predictions		Recall
Low		70					43						61.43%
Medium	69					21						30.43%
High	70					41						58.57%

The model is highly proficient at identifying the extremes (Low and High) but struggles significantly with the Medium class. This is a classic symptom of boundary fuzziness inherent in discretization. A leaf with a continuous residue of 2.56 mg/cm is labeled "Low," while a leaf with 2.58 mg/cm is labeled "Medium." 

Visually, these leaves are identical, making it nearly impossible for the network to cleanly separate the classes at the arbitrary mathematical thresholds."
