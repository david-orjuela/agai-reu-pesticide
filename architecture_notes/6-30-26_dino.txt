Exploring if DINO(v3?) can extract better features than the frozen restnet50 from the leaf images. It is a self-supervised algorithm, different from ResNet50 (imagenet). 
Its richer representations may be more valuable in this low-data area, where very few labeled examples exist.
The embeddings it generates of each leaf's visual appearance may contain information correlated with pesticide desposition.

So I'll instantiate a DINOv3 model, freeze it, and only change the downstream predictor (classification/regression head) to learn the mappings from the embeddings to ICP residue  values.

Question to answer right now: 
- Whether these DINO embeddings outperform frozen ResNet50 features
- Whether DINO improves low/med/high residue classification
- Whether DINO improves continuous regression metrics.
- Finetuning DINO vs Freezing features
