The model never predicts medium
This points to the model collapsing toward high vs not-high, and medium not being learned.


Next best steps:

1. Evaluate the best checkpoint, not just the last epoch.
2. Run repeated stratified splits and report mean ± std.
3. Try stronger regularization on the final layer:
 - weight decay
 - lower learning rate
 - fewer epochs / early stopping
4. Consider 2-bin classification as a sanity test:
 - low vs high
 - or low + medium vs high

The medium class may genuinely be visually ambiguous, especially if 600/800 overlap chemically.
