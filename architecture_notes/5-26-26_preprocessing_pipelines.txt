Preprocessing notes:

The file that links an image to its ground truth excel and maps them is complete. The only missing step is the preprocessing of the dataset image itself. That is, isolating the correct leaf and isolating it. This ensures it is trainable (does not learn background, etc.). The only issue is how to decide which leaf to isolate among many of them. In each dataset image, the ICP'd leaf is roughly center and held next to a test tube with the nominal treatment PPM written on it. 

As these images are annotated and masked on Roboflow, I am going to explore using the largest-area mask as what defines "the leaf" that is paired with the ICP measurement. Since there are only 36 images in this dataset, verifying that they are all correct should be trivial. Though, in some cases, it is hard even for me to visually identify the main leaf, given that in all images they are surrounded by various other leaves (in some cases, they are also centered and large).

Each Roboflow annotation has two classes: leaf and residue. Annotators annotated both the leaf itself and the residue on it. Since surrounding leaves were included, I cannot simply isolate the annotation. I'm not sure why these were included, but anything other than the ICP'd images would be pure noise.
